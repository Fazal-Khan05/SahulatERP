SahulatERP · Step 1

Screen mock-ups for review

Eight screens, drawn before anything is built. The data is made up (a bike-parts importer in Karachi) and nothing saves. The only question is whether the people who will use it would use it.

The screens

  1. 1 Owner home Owner Cash and bank, receivables, today's sales, approvals waiting, and what needs attention.
  2. 2 Salesperson home Salesperson My customers with balance and last order, my open orders, my drafts, new order.
  3. 3 Recovery home Recovery officer Cheques to deposit and to clear, today's follow-ups, overdue customers.
  4. 4 Warehouse home Warehouse manager Goods to receive, deliveries to pick today, stock below reorder level, transfers.
  5. 5 Accountant home Accountant Unposted drafts, bank lines to reconcile, bills due, period close, trial balance.
  6. 6 Needs attention Everyone The one inbox: everything waiting on someone, in a single table.
  7. 7 Sales invoice Whoever makes invoices The one document pattern, as a draft and as a submitted invoice.
  8. 8 Invoice list Whoever makes invoices A list page: saved filters, sort, column chooser, export.

How to run a review

  1. Sit with one reviewer and open the screen for their own job first.
  2. Say nothing for the first minute. Watch where they look and what they try to click.
  3. Ask the three questions in the yellow box at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Narrow the window to phone width once and ask whether it still makes sense.
  5. Write what they said in docs/UI-NOTES.md, under that screen's heading. Objections matter more than praise.

Step 1 is finished when two reviewers say they would use it. Buttons do nothing; filter chips, the More toggle and the Draft / Submitted switch do work.
