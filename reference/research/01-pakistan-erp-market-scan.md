# SahulatERP — Pakistan ERP/POS Market Scan
**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Purpose:** Competitive landscape, pricing, feature coverage, and gap analysis for a Pakistan-focused ERP targeting importers, distributors, wholesalers and retail chains.

---

## 1. Market segments (how the competition actually splits)

The Pakistani market is NOT one market. It splits into five tiers that barely compete with each other:

| Tier | Typical buyer | Price band | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T0 — Free / khata apps** | Single-shop kiryana, mobile shop | Rs 0 – 1,500/mo | Timeline POS (free), Udhaar Book, khata apps |
| **T1 — Cheap cloud POS/accounting** | 1–3 branch retail, small trader | Rs 1,500 – 7,000/mo | Splendid Accounts, HysabOne, Muhasib ERP, Moneypex, CloudPOS, PakERP |
| **T2 — Local "ERP" (desktop or hybrid)** | 10–100 staff trading/distribution/manufacturing | Rs 200k – 1.5M one-time, or Rs 8k–50k/mo | Candela RMS, CIS ERP, Oscar ERP, Sidat Hyder Financials, Websol, SowaanERP, Xenon |
| **T3 — Odoo / ERPNext via local partner** | Growing SME, 20–100 users | Rs 300k – 3M implementation + Rs 7k–15k/user/mo | Mantech, Pearl Solutions, CyMax, Smart Nexus, dozens of partners |
| **T4 — Enterprise** | Corporates, listed cos | Rs 5M – 15M+ implementation | SAP B1, Dynamics 365 BC, NetSuite, Systems Ltd |

**Adjacent but separate:** Distribution Management Systems (DMS) / field-force automation — Salesflo, SAMS. These sell to FMCG principals & their distributors and are *not* full ERPs. This is the segment closest to your friend's pain point.

## 2. Verified pricing (public, as of Sept 2026)

### T1 — Cloud SaaS (the volume market)

**Splendid Accounts** (Karachi) — the most transparent competitor, closest to what SahulatERP would be
- Basic: **Rs 2,000/mo** — Sales module only, POS, mobile app, 19 reports
- Standard: **Rs 3,000/mo** — Sales + Inventory, 42 reports, unlimited vendors
- Premium: **Rs 4,500/mo** — Complete ERP, CRM, manufacturing, multi-currency, 105 reports, FBR integration
- Additional user: **Rs 1,500/user/mo**
- Annual billing: 25% off (Premium = Rs 40,500/yr)
- **Add-ons (important — this is how they monetize):**
  - Order Booker App: Rs 1,030/mo
  - POS Offline: Rs 515/mo
  - Digital Invoicing (FBR): Rs 1,500–2,000/mo
  - Additional branch: Rs 1,500/mo
  - SMS packs: Rs 3,000 – 107,400
- No payroll module mentioned.

**HysabOne** — "unlimited users & branches" positioning
- Accounts: from **Rs 3,499/mo**
- POS: from **Rs 6,563/mo**
- Standard: from **Rs 7,000/mo** — inventory, sales orders, multi-store transfers, *sales agent analytics*
- Yearly saves 12.5%. Urdu/English bilingual UI. WhatsApp e-invoicing. FBR IRIS posting.
- Claims 500+ businesses.

**PakERP** — priced per seat bucket, not per module
- 1 user: **Rs 4,700/mo** | 3 users: **Rs 8,900/mo** | 5 users: **Rs 10,800/mo** | 10 users: **Rs 14,500/mo**
- 15% off annual. "All features included" at every tier.
- Custom dev: **Rs 5,000/hour**. Admin training: **Rs 50,000/month**.

**Muhasib ERP (Websol)** — from **Rs 1,500/mo**. Notable: has a *commission agent* feature (tick "Allow commission", enter a number). Basic, but it exists.

**Moneypex** — claims accounting + inventory + POS + manufacturing + **HR & payroll** + FBR digital invoicing. Pricing gated behind demo.

**CloudPOS** — pricing gated. Strong integration story: FBR + PRA/SRB/BRA/KPRA, Shopify/WooCommerce/OpenCart, Easypaisa/JazzCash/Bank Alfalah, TCS/BlueEx/Swyft/Call Courier couriers. 14+ industry verticals.

**AmalERP** — from **Rs 2,500/mo**, built-in FBR digital invoicing.

**CIS Core ERP** — **Rs 10,000/mo**, markets "implementation in 24 hours".

**General market rate for local cloud POS: Rs 2,000 – 6,000/month per branch.**

### T2 — Local ERP, one-time licence model
- Local vendor perpetual licences: **Rs 25,000 – 150,000**
- Basic custom-built POS: **from Rs 800,000**
- Multi-branch POS + ERP integration: **Rs 3,000,000+**
- Adding an FBR e-invoicing module to an existing system: **Rs 150,000 – 400,000**
- Local "true ERP" (PACT, Sage/Peachtree adaptations, custom): **Rs 200,000 – 500,000**

**Xenon ERP's published total-cost-of-ownership framing** (useful benchmark for what T2/T3 buyers expect to pay):
- Small (1–2 locations): setup Rs 300k–600k, ongoing Rs 25k–60k/mo
- Growing SME (multi-location): setup Rs 600k–1M, ongoing Rs 60k–120k/mo
- Mid-size: setup Rs 1M–1.5M, ongoing Rs 120k–250k/mo
- Data migration + training ≈ 10–15% of setup cost. Claimed ROI in 8–14 months.

**Candela RMS** (Lahore, Presidential Award winner) — the incumbent in Pakistani organised retail. Desktop + cloud. Scales from single outlet to hundreds of stores. POS, inventory, PO, warehouse, loyalty, promotions, CRM. Integrated with **PRA** for restaurant invoices and with **SAP A1/B1**. Pricing is quote-only. Separate vertical product: Candela Pharmacy.

### T3 — Odoo / ERPNext
- **Odoo Enterprise licence: ~Rs 7,000–15,000/user/month** (some partners quote Rs 20,000+/user/mo)
- Odoo implementation in Pakistan: **Rs 300,000 – 1,500,000** for a standard SME; Mantech advertises from **Rs 500,000**
- 30-user Odoo Enterprise ≈ **Rs 91,500/mo year 1**, ~Rs 114,200/mo year 2+
- Timeline: 8–14 weeks typical; 6–8 weeks for 10–15 users / 2–3 modules
- **ERPNext: Rs 0 licence.** Costs are hosting (Frappe Cloud from $5/mo) + implementation ($2,000–$100,000). Typical 10–30 user SME all-in year 1: **$10,000–$15,000**
- **SowaanERP** (ERPNext-based, Pakistan): **$300/user/year**; HR module quoted Rs 1,500–3,000/user/mo

### T4 — Enterprise
- **SAP Business One:** $1,500–$3,500/user one-time; 20-user system Rs 5M–10M+; implementation Rs 5M–15M+
- **Dynamics 365:** $70–$210/user/mo; implementation Rs 3M–8M+
- **NetSuite:** $999/mo base + $99/user/mo
- All three: **FBR compliance requires custom development** — no native Pakistani tax handling. This is a real, repeatedly-cited weakness.

## 3. The regulatory wedge: FBR Digital Invoicing

This is the single biggest market-making event for ERP in Pakistan right now, and it is *the* reason a new entrant can win in 2026.

**The mandate**
- S.R.O. 709 (22 Apr 2025) made electronic invoicing mandatory for corporate and non-corporate sales-tax-registered persons.
- Original deadlines: corporate **1 June 2025**, non-corporate **1 July 2025**. Repeatedly extended.
- Phased rollout continued under **SRO 1852(I)/2025**; a commonly cited backstop date for all active sales-tax filers is **31 July 2026**.
- By end-2025 the scope was effectively **all sales-tax-registered persons**, not just large taxpayers.
- Scope explicitly includes service sectors: restaurants, hotels, clinics, salons, couriers, online sellers.

**How it works technically**
- Every invoice must get a **unique FBR invoice number (IRN) before the sale completes**, carry a **scannable QR code**, and include seller/buyer details, tax amounts and **HS code**, transmitted in real time to FBR's server.
- Transmission must go through an **FBR-licensed integrator**.
- **STGO No. 01 of 2026** allows a business to use **multiple licensed integrators** simultaneously.
- Bona-fide corrections allowed within **72 hours**; after that, Commissioner IR approval required.

**The licensed integrators (8, per FBR's official list)**

| # | Licence No. | Entity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 398586644 | Haball (Pvt) Ltd |
| 2 | 394537651 | WebDNAWorks (Pvt) Ltd |
| 3 | 649954048 | EY Ford Rhodes |
| 4 | — | **PRAL** (govt-owned) |
| 5 | 715193372 | OpenPort Pakistan (Pvt) Ltd |
| 6 | 620903892 | TMR Consulting (Pvt) Ltd |
| 7 | 555378169 | NatureTech (Pvt) Ltd |
| 8 | 90332116 | Dynamic Resources (Pvt) Ltd |

**Economics**
- FBR charges **nothing**.
- **PRAL integrates free of cost.** This kills the "integration fee" as a business model.
- Private integrators may charge, but **fees are capped by FBR** via STGO.
- Real market cost today: software/POS setup Rs 15,000–40,000 one-time, digital certificate/device Rs 5,000–10,000, annual maintenance Rs 10,000–20,000. **First-year total Rs 15,000–60,000** for an SME.
- Competitors charge Rs 1,500–2,000/mo as an FBR add-on (Splendid).

**Penalties:** Rs 500,000 first default, escalating to Rs 3,000,000 for repeat. Finance Act 2026 adds registration-suspension and blacklisting powers. Enforcement broadened from **January 2026**.

**Strategic read:** Licensing (Chapter XIV, Sales Tax Rules 2006) is a real moat but PRAL's free service means you should *integrate through PRAL and make compliance a feature, not a product*. Selling "FBR compliance" alone is a commodity by 2027. Selling "you're compliant AND you finally know what your sales team earned" is not.

## 4. Gap analysis — where every competitor is weak

Mapped against the pain your friend described:

### Gap 1 — Commission & incentive management (the core thesis) ⭐
- **Nobody in Pakistan does this properly.** The best local offering found is Muhasib ERP's "Allow commission → enter a number" — a flat per-user percentage. HysabOne has "sales agent analytics" (reporting only, not payout calculation).
- What's actually needed and missing everywhere: multi-tier hierarchy (RSM → ASM → TSM → Order Booker), slab/tiered rates, commission on *recovery* not just invoice, target vs. achievement, product-category-specific rates, split commissions, clawback on returns and bad debt, and **the calculated commission flowing straight into payroll**.
- Global tools that do this (QuotaPath, Performio, incentX, Everstage) are all USD-priced, have zero Pakistan presence, and don't integrate with any Pakistani ERP.
- **This is a genuine, defensible wedge.** It's also the reason your friend's team is keeping a parallel Excel — which means every competitor's customer is doing the same thing.

### Gap 2 — Import landed costing
- Your friend imports from China. Almost no Pakistani T1 SaaS handles this.
- Needed: PO in USD/CNY → LC / TT / bank charges → freight → insurance → **GD (Goods Declaration)** → customs duty + regulatory duty + additional customs duty + sales tax at import + advance income tax → clearing agent bill → inland freight → **apportion all of it across SKUs by value/weight/volume** → post inventory at true landed cost per unit.
- Landed cost typically runs **15–40% above FOB**. Without this, gross margin reporting is fiction.
- International ERPs do it; Pakistani SaaS largely doesn't; T2 local ERPs do it inconsistently and usually via Excel.

### Gap 3 — Payroll that connects to sales
- Compliance pieces needed: **EOBI** (5% employer / 1% employee), **SESSI/PESSI** (provincial), provident fund, **gratuity**, income tax withholding under Income Tax Ordinance 2001 slabs, minimum wage changes.
- Dedicated HR vendors exist (PayPeople, WebHR, IceHrm, Financeora) but they are **separate products** — they don't know what an RSM sold. Moneypex claims payroll inside the ERP; worth verifying depth.
- The unmet need: **variable pay = base + commission + incentive + fuel/travel allowance, computed from live sales data, in one payslip.**

### Gap 4 — Credit control & recovery
- Pakistani wholesale runs on *udhaar*. Needed: party-wise credit limits, ageing, cheque management (PDC, bounced cheque handling), recovery officer assignment, and — critically — **commission only released once payment is recovered**.
- Rarely handled well; mostly bolted-on ageing reports.

### Gap 5 — Multi-entity / multi-currency for importers
- Import in USD/CNY, sell in PKR, with exchange-rate gain/loss on LC settlement. Splendid supports 160+ currencies nominally; realistic FX revaluation accounting is rare.

### Gap 6 — Urdu / low-literacy UX and offline-first
- HysabOne is one of the few advertising bilingual Urdu/English. Most are English-only.
- Offline POS is sold as a *paid add-on* (Splendid: Rs 515/mo) despite Pakistani power/internet reality.

### Gap 7 — Enterprise systems have no Pakistani tax brain
- SAP/Dynamics/NetSuite all require custom dev for FBR. Repeatedly cited. Opportunity for a "FBR + payroll + commission layer" even as a bolt-on.

## 5. Market context

- SMEs are **>90% of all enterprises** in Pakistan; only **~18%** have any online presence.
- Retail market CAGR forecast **8.2%** (2026–2032).
- E-commerce expected to pass **PKR 500bn (~$1.8bn)** by end-2026, growing 18–22% CAGR.
- Economy remains largely **cash-based and undocumented**; ~55% of e-commerce payments still COD.
- Bazaar Technologies raised **$70M** (Tiger Global, Dragoneer) specifically to digitise Pakistani retail — capital is flowing to this thesis.
- Implication: the FBR mandate is force-marching hundreds of thousands of previously-undocumented businesses into needing software between now and 2027. That is the demand wave to ride.

## 6. Pricing strategy implications for SahulatERP

Observed anchors:
- Sub-Rs 2,000/mo → perceived as a khata app, not an ERP. Low willingness to pay, high churn.
- **Rs 3,000–7,000/mo** → the fought-over T1 band. Splendid, HysabOne, PakERP, Muhasib all live here.
- **Rs 10,000–50,000/mo** → thin competition, mostly quote-only local vendors with poor product. Businesses like your friend's sit here and are underserved.
- Rs 90,000+/mo → Odoo Enterprise territory, where you'd have to beat a global product on features.

The add-on model (Splendid's order-booker at Rs 1,030/mo, extra branch at Rs 1,500/mo, FBR at Rs 1,500–2,000/mo) is proven in this market and worth copying — land cheap, expand via modules.

**Unaddressed sweet spot: Rs 15,000–40,000/mo for an importer/distributor with 20–60 staff and a field sales team, where commission automation alone saves a full-time person and prevents payout disputes.**
